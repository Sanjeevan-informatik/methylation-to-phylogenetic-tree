    Choose a compatible model
    DNA substitution models describe nucleotides. Binary models describe 0/1 states. Continuous beta distances produce exploratory similarity trees.
    Account for selected sites
    Variable-only likelihood inference requires ASC correction. The workbench rejects incompatible inputs instead of silently changing the model.
    Validate before claiming ancestry
    Use known-tree simulations and independent lineage evidence. Patient or tissue similarity alone does not establish cellular ancestry.
